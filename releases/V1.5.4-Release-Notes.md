# Invoice Maker v1.5.4

- Invoice item references are editable in the invoice workspace and printed in invoice PDFs. Quotation references remain editable and printed.
- Corrected invoice unit prices to two decimal places when adding Price List or stock items and when editing the invoice Price cell. Supplier Price List values remain unchanged.
- The existing quotation **Hide in PDF** checkbox continues to omit selected rows while keeping their values in the saved total.
- Service/labour lines can use a percentage of the other item lines' tax-inclusive total. The percentage is saved, recalculates when item values change, and carries from a quotation into an invoice. Fixed-price lines remain supported.
- Quotations can still convert to numbered draft invoices. A new **Export Proforma Invoice PDF** action produces a proforma from a quotation without creating a payable invoice or changing stock.
- Existing document records are preserved by the nullable `AddPercentageServiceLines` migration.

Download the self-contained Windows x64 ZIP, extract all files together, and run `InvoiceSoftware.App.exe`. Existing application data and licenses remain in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\InvoiceSoftware` and are not included in the ZIP.
